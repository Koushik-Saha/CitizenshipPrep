## What it means

The App Store and Google Play listing is the finding that matters. Both stores need a distinct app
name, and another live app called Oathly in the same stores is a source of confusion and a
possible trademark conflict, whoever filed first. Before store submission, either choose a
different name, or take advice on whether "Oathly" can be used for a citizenship-test app alongside
a habit tracker.
