# Gaps before a worldwide consumer launch

Reviewed 9 October 2026, reading the repository as a newcomer: `CLAUDE.md`, every file in
`docs/`, the 19 migrations in `db/migrations`, `apps/web`, `apps/mobile` and the packages. The
live site (`https://oathly.vercel.app`) was read for one fact only: how many published questions
each country has. Nothing was changed.

**Severity**

- **launch-blocker**: the product cannot be sold, cannot pass store review, or is exposed
  legally or financially without it.
- **high**: launch is possible, but a paying learner, a regulator or the first incident will find it.
- **low**: worth doing; nobody is harmed if it waits.

**Effort:** S is under a day, M is one to three days, L is a week or more (or waits on other
people).

**How far to trust this.** Every row marked "read" was confirmed in the code or the schema. Rows
marked "not evidenced" are settings that live in a console (Neon, Vercel, Anthropic, Stripe) and
could not be seen from the repository: they may already be done. Individual screens were checked
by reading their code, not by using them, and no screen reader was used.

"The database" below is Neon. The request named Supabase backups; this project left Supabase
some time ago (`docs/launch.md`, section 0).

## Checked and found in place

So that these are not looked for twice:

- **Delete account** on the website and in the phone app, without writing to anyone, with a public
  page for the stores (`/delete-account`).
- **Privacy policy and terms** exist, name the operator and list every company that handles data.
- **Not affiliated with any government** on every public page, sign-in, plans and the phone's
  first screen; the tutor and AI explanations say they are AI and can be wrong; study groups hold
  posts about a person's own case and add a "see a licensed attorney" notice.
- **Unpublished questions are not served**: the public pages, sessions, explanations and the
  tutor all read on `status = 'published'`.
- **Per-learner limits** on AI use and on sign-in, posting and reporting (`docs/security.md`).
- **SEO**: canonical links, alternate-language links, sitemap, robots, structured data, a 404 page
  and an error page in the reader's language; Lighthouse SEO of 100 is enforced in CI.
- **App privacy answers and the Data safety form** are drafted in `docs/store/listing.md` and
  match the privacy manifest in `apps/mobile/app.json`.
- **Restore purchases** is in the phone app's plans screen. The phone app offers no third-party
  sign-in, so "Sign in with Apple" is not required.
- **Contrast** is tested in `packages/tokens`; reduced motion is respected. There is no video, so
  nothing needs captions; audio mode reads text that is already on the screen.
