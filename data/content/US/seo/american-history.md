# American history on the U.S. citizenship test

- **Title:** American history on the U.S. citizenship test | Oathly
- **Description:** What the 2025 U.S. civics test asks about American history, from the colonies and independence to September 11, 2001.

## Introduction

About a third of the official question list is history, in three periods: the colonies and independence, the 1800s, and the 1900s to today.

## What the test asks

- **46 of the 128 official questions** are history: 17 on the colonial period and independence, 10 on the 1800s, and 19 on recent history. [1]

## Key facts

- England's first permanent settlement, Jamestown, was created in 1607. [2, chapter 7]
- The Declaration of Independence was adopted on July 4, 1776; Thomas Jefferson wrote it. [2, chapter 8]
- The Revolutionary War was fought from 1775 to 1783. [2, chapter 8]
- The United States bought the Louisiana Territory from France in 1803. [2, chapter 9]
- The Civil War was fought between the North and the South from 1861 to 1865, and the 13th Amendment then made slavery illegal. [2, chapter 10]
- Franklin Roosevelt was President during the Great Depression and World War II. [2, chapter 11]
- The civil rights movement worked to end racial discrimination; Congress passed the Civil Rights Act in 1964 and the Voting Rights Act in 1965. [2, chapter 11]
