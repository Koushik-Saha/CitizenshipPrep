## Introduction

This is the shortest part of the official question list, and the quickest to learn.

## What the test asks

- **10 of the 128 official questions** are here: 6 on symbols and 4 on holidays. [1]

## Key facts

- The flag has 13 stripes for the 13 original colonies and 50 stars, one for each state. [2, chapter 12]
- The national anthem is "The Star-Spangled Banner", written by Francis Scott Key. [2, chapter 12]
- The Statue of Liberty is on Liberty Island in New York Harbor; France gave it to the United States in 1886. [2, chapter 12]
- Independence Day is July 4. Memorial Day, the last Monday in May, honors those who died in service; Veterans Day, November 11, honors everyone who has served. [2, chapter 12]
- Juneteenth, June 19, recognizes the end of slavery in the United States. [2, chapter 12]
