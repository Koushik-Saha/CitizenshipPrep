# United Kingdom: topics of the Life in the UK Test

Exam version: the **Life in the UK Test** as described on GOV.UK (`life-in-the-uk-test`): 24
questions in 45 minutes, 75% to pass. Map written 9 October 2026.

## The syllabus is not in this pack

The test is based only on the official handbook, _Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New
Residents_ (`handbook`). GOV.UK says: "You’ll only be tested on information from the official
Guide for New Residents." The handbook is sold, not published free, and was not bought. So:

- its chapters are **not listed here**, because they were not read from an official source;
- the weight of each topic is **not published** on GOV.UK and **cannot be estimated** without the
  book;
- no question in `data/questions/GB/` is taken from it.

| Topic in the app       | Share of the test | Basis | Where it comes from                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Government and the Law | not known         | none  | No source in the pack gives or implies one |
| History                | not known         | none  | No source in the pack gives or implies one |
| Nations and Traditions | not known         | none  | No source in the pack gives or implies one |

## What the questions rest on instead

Until the handbook is in the pack, questions are written only from GOV.UK pages published under
the Open Government Licence v3.0, on civic subjects a test about life in the UK is likely to
cover (GOV.UK describes the test as being about "British traditions and customs"):

| Source                   | Subject                                                  | Topic in the app       |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| `how-government-works`   | Prime Minister, Cabinet, Civil Service, laws, devolution | Government and the Law |
| `elections-in-the-uk`    | Who can vote, voting systems, the devolved legislatures  | Government and the Law |
| `jury-service`           | Jury service in England and Wales                        | Government and the Law |
| `how-your-council-works` | Types of council in England                              | Government and the Law |
| `bank-holidays`          | Which nation keeps which bank holiday                    | Nations and Traditions |

Nothing is written for History: no openly licensed official page in the pack covers it.

## For the reviewer

Every question here is true to the GOV.UK page it cites, but that does **not** make it a test
question. Before publishing any of them, check it against the current handbook:

- drop a question if the fact is not in the handbook;
- where the handbook gives a different figure from GOV.UK (the number of members of a devolved
  legislature, an age limit), the test follows the handbook. Those questions are marked for a
  freshness check.
