# Australia: topics of the citizenship test

Exam version: **Australian citizenship test as changed on 15 November 2020**, when five questions
on Australian values were added. Its only study text is the testable section of _Australian
Citizenship: Our Common Bond_ (`our-common-bond`). Map written 9 October 2026.

Topics are the four parts of the testable section, in its order. Subtopics are the headings inside
each part.

## Weights

One share is published. The booklet says the test has 20 questions, five of them on Australian
values (page 3), and the practice test's opening page (`practice-test`) says the same: "answer 5/5
(100%) of the Australian values questions correctly" and "at least 15/20 (75%)". So Part 4 is a
quarter of the test.

How the other 15 questions are spread across Parts 1 to 3 is **not published**. Those shares are
an **estimate**: each part's share of the text of Parts 1 to 3, counted in characters from the
downloaded copy, applied to the remaining 75%.

| Topic                                    | Part of the booklet | Pages (printed) | Share of the test | Basis     | Subtopics                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------- | ----------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Australia and Its People                 | Part 1              | 8 to 16         | 24.2%             | estimate  | Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples; Early days of European settlement; The nation of Australia; States and territories; Traditions; Important days; Flags; Symbols; The national anthem |
| Democratic Beliefs, Rights and Liberties | Part 2              | 18 to 22        | 18.6%             | estimate  | Our democratic beliefs; Our freedoms; Our equalities; Responsibilities and privileges of citizenship; Participating in Australian society                                                          |

The shares add up to 100%.

## What the weights do not capture

- **The values questions decide the result.** All five must be right. A candidate who scores 19
  out of 20 but misses one values question fails, so Part 4 matters more than a quarter.
- **The glossary** (pages 40 to 43) is testable too. It defines words used in Parts 1 to 4 and is
  not counted as a topic of its own.
- **Facts that change.** The booklet names the Head of State and gives the number of senators.
  Questions on those are marked for a freshness check.
