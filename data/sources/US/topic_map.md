# United States: topics of the civics test

Exam version: **2025 Naturalization Civics Test** (128 questions, form M-1778 09/25), for Form
N-400 filed on or after 20 October 2025. Map written 9 October 2026.

Topics and subtopics are the headings of the official question list, in its order
(`civics-questions-2025`). The study guide (`study-guide-2025`) is arranged differently, in twelve
chapters; the last column says where each topic is taught.

## Weights

USCIS does not publish how the 20 questions asked at an interview are chosen from the 128. The
format page (`civics-test-2025-page`) says only "an oral test consisting of 20 questions from the
list of 128 civics test questions", and the Federal Register notice (`federal-register-notice`)
adds no weighting.

So every share below is an **estimate**: the topic's share of the 128-question list, on the
assumption that any question is as likely to be asked as any other. Counted by reading the list.

## What the weights do not capture

- **The 65/20 list.** Applicants aged 65 or over with 20 years as a permanent resident are asked
  10 questions from the 20 marked with an asterisk, and pass with 6. Those 20 are spread across
  all three topics.
- **Answers that depend on where the applicant lives.** The questions on the applicant's
  senators, representative, governor and state capital have no single answer. There are no
  regional versions of the test itself.
- **Answers that change.** Officeholders change with elections and appointments; `test-updates` is
  the page USCIS keeps current.
- **Geography.** The study guide has a chapter on U.S. geography (chapter 6), but the 2025 list
  has no geography section; what the guide teaches there supports questions filed under the other
  headings.
