# Canada: topics of the citizenship test

Exam version: the citizenship test as described on IRCC's "Study for the test" page
(`study-for-the-test`, dated 31 March 2026): 20 questions, 15 to pass. Its only study text is
_Discover Canada_ (`discover-canada`, Ci1-11/2021E-PDF). Map written 9 October 2026.

Subtopics are the chapters of the guide, in its order. They are grouped under four topics. Three
are the ones the app already had for Canada; "Rights and Responsibilities" is added because the
test page names it first among what is asked.

## Weights

IRCC **does not publish** how the 20 questions are spread. "Study for the test" lists the subjects
(rights and responsibilities, history, geography, economy, government, laws, symbols) without
numbers, and section 15 of the Citizenship Regulations, reprinted on page 64 of the guide, lists
subjects too.

So every share below is an **estimate**: the chapter's share of the guide's text from page 8 to
page 51, counted in characters from the downloaded copy.

By topic: Rights and Responsibilities 4.6%, History 47.9%, Government 20.0%, Geography and Symbols
27.4%. The shares add up to 100% (99.9% after rounding).

## What the weights do not capture

- **The guide is out of date in places.** It names Queen Elizabeth II as Sovereign, gives 308
  electoral districts, a population of about 34 million, three major parties and the G8 with
  Russia. No question is written on those points. Questions that rest on a figure from the guide
  are marked for a freshness check.
- **Questions about where the applicant lives.** Pages 34 and 35 ask for the applicant's own MP,
  premier, lieutenant governor and capital. Those have no single answer and no question covers
  them yet.
- **Rights and Responsibilities is short in the guide** but named first on the test page, so its
  true share is probably higher than 4.6%.
