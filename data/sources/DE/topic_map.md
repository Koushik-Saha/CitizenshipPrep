# Germany: topics of the Einbürgerungstest

Exam version: **Einbürgerungstestverordnung (EinbTestV), Annex 1**, as last amended by the
regulation of 21 June 2024 (`einbtestv`). Map written 9 October 2026. The test is in German.

## Topics

The annex's preface names three subject areas for the 300 general questions, plus ten questions
for each Land:

| Topic in the app             | Named in the annex               | Questions                    |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Politik in der Demokratie    | „Leben in der Demokratie“        | Part I, among numbers 1–300  |
| Geschichte und Verantwortung | „Geschichte und Verantwortung“   | Part I, among numbers 1–300  |
| Mensch und Gesellschaft      | „Mensch und Gesellschaft“        | Part I, among numbers 1–300  |
| (the applicant's Land)       | „landesbezogene Fragen“, Part II | 10 for each of the 16 Länder |

The first topic keeps the name the app already used; the annex calls it „Leben in der
Demokratie“. The annex does **not** say which question belongs to which area. The topic of each
imported question is Oathly's own reading of it.

## What is imported, and what is not

- **Wording.** The catalogue is an annex to a federal regulation. Section 5(1) of the Copyright
  Act (`urhg-5`) says regulations have no copyright, so the questions and their four answers are
  copied exactly, with their numbers.
- **Answers.** The annex prints **no answer key**, and neither does BAMF's PDF
  (`bamf-gesamtfragenkatalog`). The only official place the answers appear is BAMF's interactive
  test centre (`online-testcenter`), which is copyrighted and was not harvested. So a question is
  imported only where a law in this pack settles its answer in so many words: the Basic Law
  (`grundgesetz`) or the Unification Treaty (`einigungsvertrag`). Each explanation names the
  article, and `review.csv` quotes the words.
- **67 of the 300** general questions meet that test so far. The other 233, and all 160 Land
  questions, are not imported. They need either the official key, checked question by question, or
  a primary source for each answer (history and everyday-life questions have none in the pack).
- **BAMF's edition of 7 May 2025** has the same questions under the same numbers but edits the
  wording slightly (it puts the feminine form first). The regulation's wording is the one used. A
  reviewer should confirm no question has been replaced since the 2024 amendment.

## For the reviewer

A native German speaker has to sign off every question. Check each answer against BAMF's test
centre before publishing: the law settles it, but the key is BAMF's.
