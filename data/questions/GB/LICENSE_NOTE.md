# United Kingdom: no official questions imported

Checked 9 October 2026. There is no `official.json` for the United Kingdom.

- **No official questions are published.** GOV.UK says the 24 questions are based on the official
  handbook and publishes none of them. Official practice questions exist only as products of the
  official shop (an app and an e-learning subscription).
- **The handbook is not in the pack.** _Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents_ is
  sold as a book, an eBook and in audio. No free official copy exists, and it was not bought, so
  its licence terms have not been read. It is recorded as `reuse_unclear` in
  `data/sources/GB/sources.json`.

The questions here are written by Oathly from GOV.UK pages under the Open Government Licence
v3.0, which allows commercial reuse with attribution ("Contains public sector information
licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0"). They cover government, elections, juries,
councils and bank holidays only. They have **not** been checked against the handbook: see
`data/sources/GB/topic_map.md`.

To do this properly, buy the current handbook, add it to the pack with its imprint page read for
the licence, and write and check questions against it.
