# Canada: no official questions imported

Checked 9 October 2026. There is no `official.json` for Canada.

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada publishes no list of the test's questions. It does
publish sample "study questions" in _Discover Canada_ (pages 52 and 53, and online), a few of them
with answers. Those are not imported, because the Government of Canada's terms for Canada.ca say:

> Unless otherwise specified, you may not reproduce materials on this site, in whole or in part,
> for the purposes of commercial redistribution without prior written permission from the
> copyright administrator.

<https://www.canada.ca/en/transparency/terms.html>. Reproduction for non-commercial purposes is
allowed without permission; Oathly is a paid app, so that does not apply. The sources are recorded
as `all_rights_reserved`, commercial use `not_allowed`, in `data/sources/CA/sources.json`.

Every question here is written by Oathly in its own words. Facts are taken from the guide; each
question cites its page and quotes fewer than 25 words to show where the answer is. No question
copies the wording or the answer choices of an official sample question.

To change this, write to IRCC for permission to reproduce the study questions commercially, and
record the answer in `sources.json`.
