# Australia: no official questions imported

Checked 9 October 2026. There is no `official.json` for Australia, for two reasons.

1. **No official question list exists.** The Department of Home Affairs publishes the study
   booklet but not the test's questions. Its "Prepare for the citizenship test" page says only that
   "All test questions are based on the OCB resource booklet."
2. **The practice test states no licence.** The Department runs an interactive practice test of 20
   questions at <https://citizenshippracticetest.homeaffairs.gov.au/>. Its page carries no
   copyright notice and no link to one. The Department's website terms (Creative Commons
   Attribution 3.0 Australia) speak of "this website", and the practice test is on a separate
   address, so they do not settle it. It is recorded as `reuse_unclear` in
   `data/sources/AU/sources.json` and nothing was copied from it.

Every question here is written by Oathly from the booklet, which is published under Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International (© Commonwealth of Australia 2020). Each one cites the page
it rests on and quotes fewer than 25 words from it.

To change this, ask the Department in writing whether the practice test's questions may be
reused, and record the answer in `sources.json`.
